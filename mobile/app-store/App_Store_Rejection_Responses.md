# App Store Rejection Response Templates - Timbuktoo Mobile

**Last Updated**: 2024-07-16
**Purpose**: Pre-written responses for common App Store and Google Play rejection reasons
**Status**: Production-Ready

This document provides ready-to-use response templates for the most common rejection reasons from Apple App Store and Google Play Store, specifically for AI-powered apps with subscriptions.

---

## How to Use This Document

**When You Receive a Rejection**:

1. **Read the Rejection Email**: Apple/Google will cite specific guideline violations
2. **Find the Matching Template**: Search this document for the guideline number (e.g., "5.1.1")
3. **Customize the Response**: Fill in bracketed placeholders [like this]
4. **Submit via App Store Connect**: Resolution Center → "Submit an Appeal" or "Provide Additional Information"
5. **Resubmit Build**: If code changes required, upload new build and reference changes in notes

**Response Tone**:
- Professional and respectful
- Acknowledge the concern
- Provide specific evidence (screenshots, documentation)
- Reference exact guideline numbers
- Offer to make changes if needed

## Apple App Store Rejection Responses

### Rejection 1: AI Disclosure (Guideline 5.1.1)

**Rejection Message**:
```
Your app uses AI-generated content but does not clearly disclose this to users.

Guideline 5.1.1 - Legal - Privacy - Data Collection and Storage
Apps that use AI to generate content must clearly disclose this to users and
provide mechanisms for users to report issues.
```

**Response Template**:
```

to users in multiple locations:

1. ONBOARDING DISCLOSURE (Mandatory):
   - Screen 2 of onboarding displays "AI Transparency" screen
   - Users must check "I understand itineraries are AI-generated" checkbox
   - Users CANNOT proceed without explicit consent
   - Screenshot: [Attach AIDisclosureScreen.png showing unchecked checkbox]

   - Every itinerary displays yellow banner: "⚠️ This itinerary was generated by AI"
   - Banner appears at top of itinerary, visible before any content
   - Screenshot: [Attach ItineraryScreen.png showing AI banner]

3. FEEDBACK MECHANISM (Required by Guideline 5.1.1):
   - Users can tap "Give Feedback" on every itinerary
   - Report types include: Inaccurate, Inappropriate, Missing Information, Other
   - All reports reviewed by compliance team within 48 hours
   - Screenshot: [Attach FeedbackScreen.png showing report options]

4. TERMS OF SERVICE (Liability Limitation):
   - "Timbuktoo provides AI-generated travel recommendations for informational

   - Link: https://timbuktoo.ai/terms

AI recommendations are informational only and may be inaccurate or incomplete.
Users are informed during onboarding and within the app that they must verify

additional clarification is needed.

### Rejection 3: Data Usage & Privacy (Guideline 5.1.1)

**Rejection Message**:
```
Your app's privacy practices do not match the disclosures in your Privacy Policy
or Privacy Nutrition Label.

Guideline 5.1.1 - Legal - Privacy - Data Collection and Storage
Apps must accurately disclose data collection practices.
```

**Response Template**:
```
Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Timbuktoo's privacy practices.

We respectfully submit that Timbuktoo's data collection practices MATCH our
Privacy Policy and Privacy Nutrition Label exactly:

1. DATA COLLECTED (Privacy Nutrition Label - Declared):

   a) Email Address (Contact Info):
      - Purpose: Account creation, authentication
      - Linked to User: YES
      - Used for Tracking: NO
      - How Used: JWT authentication, password reset emails only

   b) Travel Preferences (User Content):
      - Purpose: Personalization (itinerary generation)
      - Linked to User: YES
      - Used for Tracking: NO
      - How Used: Stored in PostgreSQL, used only for AI itinerary generation

   c) AI-Generated Itineraries (User Content):
      - Purpose: App functionality (save/view itineraries)
      - Linked to User: YES
      - Used for Tracking: NO
      - How Used: Generated via Claude API, stored for user access

   d) Usage Data (Product Interaction):
      - Purpose: Analytics (app improvement)
      - Linked to User: NO (aggregated only)
      - Used for Tracking: NO
      - How Used: Firebase Analytics (crash-free rate, session duration)

2. DATA NOT COLLECTED (Privacy Nutrition Label - Not Declared):
   - ❌ Location data (GPS)
   - ❌ Contacts, Photos, Camera, Microphone
   - ❌ Payment information (Stripe/Apple handles IAP)
   - ❌ Device IDs or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
   - ❌ Browsing history or search history

   - ✅ Data encrypted at rest (AES-256, AWS RDS)
   - ✅ User can request data deletion (Settings → Delete My Data, 30-day SLA)

Please let us know if additional clarification is needed.

### Rejection 4: Spam - Low Quality (Guideline 4.3)

**Rejection Message**:
```
Your app appears to be a wrapper around AI-generated content with minimal
functionality.

Guideline 4.3 - Design - Spam
Apps that are primarily AI-generated content with minimal unique functionality
may be rejected.
```

**Response Template**:
```
Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for your feedback on Timbuktoo's functionality.

We respectfully submit that Timbuktoo is NOT a simple AI wrapper, but a
sophisticated travel planning application with significant unique functionality:

1. MULTI-AGENT AI WORKFLOW (Proprietary):

   Timbuktoo uses a custom multi-agent AI architecture (not available in
   generic AI apps):

   a) City Selection Agent:
      - Analyzes user preferences (8 interest categories, food, budget, dates)
      - Recommends top 3 cities with reasoning
      - Filters 500+ destinations based on compatibility

   b) Local Expert Agent:
      - Researches each city (restaurants, attractions, events)
      - Validates availability and pricing
      - Incorporates real-time data (weather, seasonality)

   c) Concierge Agent:
      - Generates day-by-day itinerary (meals, activities, logistics)
      - Optimizes for travel time and geographic clustering
      - Balances interests across multi-day trips

   This workflow takes 30-60 seconds and involves 3 sequential AI calls with
   custom prompts, validation, and post-processing.

2. UNIQUE FEATURES (Not Available in Generic AI Apps):

   - ✅ City recommendation engine (AI suggests destinations, not just itineraries)
   - ✅ Multi-day trip planning (3-10 day itineraries with daily structure)
   - ✅ Budget-aware recommendations (Low/Medium/High/Luxury filtering)
   - ✅ Food preference integration (Adventurous/Vegetarian/Vegan/Dietary)
   - ✅ Weather context (seasonal recommendations, activity adjustments)
   - ✅ Feedback loop (users report issues, we improve recommendations)
   - ✅ Offline access (save itineraries for offline viewing)
   - ✅ Export to Calendar (coming soon - Phase 2)

3. TECHNICAL COMPLEXITY:

   - 47 source files (React Native, TypeScript, FastAPI backend)
   - 10 screens with custom UI (not generic chat interface)
   - PostgreSQL database (user accounts, preferences, itineraries, feedback)
   - JWT authentication with secure token storage (Keychain/Keystore)
   - In-app purchase integration (subscriptions, restore purchases)
   - Crash reporting and monitoring (Firebase Crashlytics)
   - SOC-2 compliant infrastructure (AWS, TLS 1.2+, AES-256)

4. USER VALUE PROPOSITION:

   Timbuktoo saves users 5-10 hours of travel research by:
   - Eliminating manual Google searches for restaurants, attractions, logistics
   - Providing structured, day-by-day plans (not unstructured AI responses)
   - Tailoring recommendations to individual preferences (not generic suggestions)
   - Validating AI outputs for quality and accuracy

We believe Timbuktoo provides substantial unique value beyond generic AI
content generation and complies with Guideline 4.3.

Please let us know if additional clarification is needed.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Timbuktoo Engineering Team
```

## Escalation & Appeal Process

### If First Response Rejected

**Wait 24-48 Hours**:
- Apple/Google may take time to review your response
- Don't submit multiple responses immediately

**Request Clarification**:
```
Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for your continued review of Timbuktoo.

We have addressed [Guideline X] by [specific action taken], but our app was
rejected again.

Could you please provide specific clarification on what additional changes are
needed to comply with [Guideline X]?

We are committed to meeting all App Store guidelines and would appreciate
detailed feedback so we can resolve this quickly.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Offer to Schedule Call**:
```
If a written explanation is insufficient, we would be happy to schedule a call
to demonstrate the app's compliance with [Guideline X].

Please let us know if this would be helpful.
```

### If Multiple Rejections

**Escalate to App Review Board** (Apple Only):
1. App Store Connect → Resolution Center
2. Click "Request Expedited Review" or "Appeal to App Review Board"
3. Explain:
   - Why your app complies with guidelines
   - Evidence you've provided
   - Impact of rejection on your business

**Contact Google Play Support** (Google Only):
1. Google Play Console → Support
2. Select "Policy and Copyright" → "Appeal"
3. Provide evidence and request human review

## Pre-Submission Checklist (Avoid Rejections)

**Before Submitting to App Store**:

- [ ] AI disclosure on onboarding (mandatory checkbox)
- [ ] AI banner on every AI-generated content screen
- [ ] Feedback/report mechanism on every itinerary
- [ ] Auto-renewal disclosure on paywall
- [ ] "Restore Purchases" button in Settings
- [ ] Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links functional
- [ ] Privacy Nutrition Label matches actual data collection
- [ ] Data Safety form (Google Play) matches actual data collection
- [ ] Demo account tested and working
- [ ] App Review Notes include complete testing flow
- [ ] All screenshots show real app UI (no mockups)
- [ ] Free tier accessible without forcing subscription
