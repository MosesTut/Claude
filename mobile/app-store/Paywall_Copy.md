## Compliance & Legal Copy

### Auto-Renewal Disclosure (REQUIRED by Apple & Google)

**iOS Version**:
```
Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
Subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the
end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24
hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel
subscriptions in your App Store account settings.
```

**Android Version**:
```
Payment will be charged to your Google account at confirmation of purchase.
Subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the
end of the current period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your
Google Play account settings.
```

**Placement**: Bottom of paywall screen, small font (10-12pt), gray text
**Required**: YES (Apple will reject without this)

### Free Trial Disclosure (If Applicable)

```
7-day free trial, then $9.99/month or $79.99/year. Cancel anytime.
```

**Placement**: Below subscription card pricing
**Required**: YES if offering free trial

### Privacy & Terms Links (REQUIRED)

```
By subscribing, you agree to our [Terms of Service] and [Privacy Policy].
```

**Links**:
- Terms of Service: https://timbuktoo.ai/terms
- Privacy Policy: https://timbuktoo.ai/privacy

**Placement**: Above compliance footer
**Required**: YES (Apple 3.1.2)

## Conversion Optimization Checklist

**Before Launching Paywall**:

- [ ] Headline clearly states value proposition
- [ ] Subheadline explains core benefit (unlimited itineraries)
- [ ] Benefits list shows 4 clear value props
- [ ] Annual plan listed first (higher LTV)
- [ ] "BEST VALUE" badge on annual plan
- [ ] Per-month pricing shown for annual ($6.67/month)
- [ ] Primary CTA emphasizes free trial (if applicable)
- [ ] Secondary CTA provides easy exit
- [ ] Legal compliance footer included (auto-renewal language)
- [ ] Terms and Privacy Policy links included
- [ ] Free trial disclosure included (if applicable)
- [ ] "Restore Purchases" link in footer or Settings
- [ ] Close button [X] in top-right corner
- [ ] Paywall dismissible (not mandatory to proceed)

**A/B Testing Recommendations**:

1. **Headline**: "Unlock Unlimited Travel Planning" vs. "Plan Unlimited Trips with AI"
2. **Benefits**: Short form (4 bullets) vs. Long form (4 bullets with descriptions)
3. **Pricing**: Annual first vs. Monthly first
4. **CTA**: "Start Free Trial" vs. "Try Pro Free for 7 Days"
5. **Badge**: "BEST VALUE" vs. "MOST POPULAR" vs. "SAVE 33%"

**Metrics to Track**:
- Paywall view rate (% of users who see paywall)
- Trial start rate (% who tap "Start Free Trial")
- Conversion rate (% who complete purchase)
- Revenue per visitor (total revenue ÷ paywall views)
- Annual vs. Monthly selection rate

## App Store Rejection Avoidance

### Common Rejection Reasons (Subscriptions)

**Rejection 1**: Misleading pricing (e.g., "Only $0.99!" when it's $9.99)
**Fix**: Always show full price, never use "only" or deceptive language

**Rejection 2**: Missing auto-renewal disclosure
**Fix**: Include full auto-renewal language (see Compliance section above)

**Rejection 3**: Forcing users to subscribe before using free features
**Fix**: Paywall must be dismissible, free tier must be accessible

**Rejection 4**: Not offering "Restore Purchases" option
**Fix**: Add "Restore Purchases" link in paywall footer or Settings

**Rejection 5**: Free trial not clearly disclosed
**Fix**: Show "7-day free trial, then $9.99/month" below pricing

### Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] Paywall includes "X" close button (not forced)
- [ ] Free tier accessible without subscribing
- [ ] Auto-renewal disclosure present and accurate
- [ ] Terms of Service and Privacy Policy linked
- [ ] Free trial duration clearly stated (if applicable)
- [ ] "Restore Purchases" option available
- [ ] Pricing matches App Store Connect configuration
- [ ] No misleading language ("free" when it's a trial)
- [ ] Legal team reviewed all copy
