# Screenshot Captions & Overlays - Timbuktoo Mobile

**Last Updated**: 2024-07-16
**Purpose**: Store-optimized captions and overlays for iOS App Store and Google Play Store screenshots
**Status**: Production-Ready

This document provides exact overlay text and captions for all 5 required App Store screenshots, optimized for conversion and App Store approval.

---

## Usage Instructions

**Design Requirements**:
- **Overlays**: Large, bold text displayed over the screenshot (40-60pt font, white text with 50% black gradient background)
- **Captions**: Smaller text below the overlay (24-32pt font, white or black depending on background)
- **Placement**: Overlay at top 1/3 of screenshot, caption in bottom 1/3
- **Font**: San Francisco (iOS), Roboto (Android), or brand font if available
- **Branding**: Timbuktoo logo in top-right corner (optional but recommended)

**Production Tools**:
- Figma (recommended): Use screenshot frame templates
- Sketch: Import screenshots, add text overlays
- Canva: Use "App Screenshot" templates
- Adobe Photoshop: Manual overlay creation

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### iOS App Store

**Screenshot Sizes** (iPhone 6.7" display - required):
- Dimensions: 1290 × 2796 pixels
- Format: PNG or JPEG (PNG recommended for text clarity)
- Color Profile: sRGB or Display P3
- File Size: Max 500KB per screenshot

**Overlay Specifications**:
- Font: San Francisco Display (system font)
- Font Weight: Bold or Heavy
- Font Size: 48-60pt
- Text Color: White (#FFFFFF) or brand color
- Background: 50% black gradient (rgba(0,0,0,0.5) to rgba(0,0,0,0.0))
- Text Shadow: 2px drop shadow for readability

**Caption Specifications**:
- Font: San Francisco Text
- Font Weight: Regular or Medium
- Font Size: 28-32pt
- Max Length: 160 characters (App Store Connect limit)
- Color: White or black (high contrast)

### Google Play Store

**Screenshot Sizes**:
- Dimensions: 1080 × 1920 pixels (minimum)
- Format: PNG or JPEG (24-bit PNG recommended)
- Max File Size: 8MB per screenshot

**Overlay Specifications**:
- Font: Roboto (Google's Material Design font)
- Font Weight: Bold
- Font Size: 40-50pt
- Same color and background guidelines as iOS

**Caption Specifications**:
- Captions are OPTIONAL on Google Play (screenshots are standalone)
- If using captions, embed directly in screenshot image
- Max recommendation: 120 characters for mobile readability

## A/B Testing Recommendations

### Headline Variations (Screenshot 1)

**Current**: "Your AI Travel Concierge"
**Test A**: "Plan Smarter Trips with AI"
**Test B**: "Personalized Travel, Powered by AI"

**Hypothesis**: "Plan Smarter Trips" may convert better by emphasizing the benefit (smarter) over the feature (AI concierge)

### Caption Variations (Screenshot 4)

**Current**: "Clear plans for every day of your trip."
**Test A**: "Never wonder what to do next."
**Test B**: "Every meal, activity, and tip—planned for you."
**Test C**: "Stress-free travel planning, day by day."

**Hypothesis**: Benefit-focused captions ("never wonder") may outperform feature descriptions

**Current**: Warm gradient (orange to yellow)
**Test A**: Cool gradient (blue to purple) - may feel more premium
**Test B**: Brand gradient (teal to blue) - may improve brand recall
**Test C**: No gradient, solid color with 60% opacity - may improve readability

**Hypothesis**: Brand colors may improve recognition, but warm colors may convert better for travel

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't**:
1. Obscure the AI disclosure checkbox in Screenshot 1
2. Hide the AI banner in Screenshot 4
3. Use generic stock photos or competitor screenshots
4. Include user-generated content without consent
5. Show placeholder text ("Lorem Ipsum", "Test User")
6. Use low-resolution images (blurry text)
7. Overload screenshots with too much overlay text
8. Violate Apple's trademark guidelines (don't say "Best App on iOS")

✅ **Do**:
1. Use real app UI from production builds
2. Show realistic travel data (real cities, restaurants)
3. Ensure text is readable on mobile devices (test on phone)
4. Keep overlays concise (4-6 words max)
5. Use high contrast (white text on dark gradient, or vice versa)
6. Test screenshots on actual devices before uploading
7. Get legal approval for AI disclosure language
8. Follow Apple's App Store Screenshot guidelines
