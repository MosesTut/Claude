## Section 7: Additional Disclosures

### AI Transparency (Critical)

**AI Usage Disclosure:**

"This app uses artificial intelligence to generate travel recommendations. AI-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete."

**User Consent:**
- Users are informed during onboarding that itineraries are AI-generated
- Mandatory consent checkbox: "I understand itineraries are AI-generated"
- Every itinerary displays banner: "⚠️ This itinerary was generated by AI"

**Content Moderation:**
- Users can report inaccurate or inappropriate content via in-app feedback
- All reports reviewed by compliance team within 48 hours
- Inaccurate content removed from knowledge base
- Inappropriate content flagged and escalated immediately

**Liability Limitation:**
- All recommendations are informational only
- Timbuktoo does not guarantee accuracy, pricing, availability, or safety
- Users are advised to independently verify all recommendations

---

### Data Retention

**Retention Periods:**

| Data Type | Retention Period | Deletion Method |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| Account data (email) | Until account deletion | User-initiated (Settings) or automated (30 days after deletion request) |
| Travel preferences | Until account deletion | User-initiated or automated |
| AI-generated itineraries | 2 years after last login | Automated deletion (PostgreSQL job) |
| User feedback | 5 years | Automated deletion (compliance requirement) |
| Usage logs | 90 days | Automated deletion (Datadog) |
| Crash logs | 90 days | Automated deletion |

**GDPR/CCPA Compliance:**
- Users can request data deletion at any time
- Deletion completed within 30 days
- Confirmation email sent upon completion
- Audit trail maintained for compliance
