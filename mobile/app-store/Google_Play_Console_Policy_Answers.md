## AI-Generated Content

### Does your app use AI or machine learning?
**Answer**: ✅ Yes

### How is AI used in your app?
**Answer**:
```
AI is used to generate personalized travel itineraries and recommendations based on user preferences.

Specifically:
1. City recommendations: AI suggests destinations based on user interests, food preferences, and budget
2. Itinerary generation: AI creates day-by-day trip plans with restaurants, attractions, and activities
3. Content curation: AI selects and organizes travel recommendations

AI model: Anthropic Claude (Claude 3.5 Sonnet)
```

### Is user disclosure provided about AI-generated content?
**Answer**: ✅ Yes

### How are users informed about AI-generated content?
**Answer**:
```
Users are informed about AI-generated content in multiple ways:

1. Onboarding disclosure (mandatory):
   - During onboarding, users must check a consent checkbox: "I understand itineraries are AI-generated"
   - Users cannot proceed without explicit consent

2. In-app disclosure:
   - Every AI-generated itinerary displays a yellow banner: "⚠️ This itinerary was generated by AI"
   - Banner appears at the top of every itinerary screen

3. Informational disclaimer:
   - Terms of Service state: "Timbuktoo provides AI-generated travel recommendations for informational purposes only. Timbuktoo does not guarantee accuracy, availability, pricing, or safety of any recommendation."

All AI recommendations are marked as informational and users are encouraged to verify details before booking.
```

### What user controls are provided for AI-generated content?
**Answer**:
```
Users have the following controls:

1. Feedback mechanism:
   - Users can rate itineraries as "Helpful" or "Not Helpful"
   - Users can report issues with 4 report types:
     • Inaccurate information
     • Inappropriate content
     • Missing key information
     • Other issue

2. Content moderation:
   - All reports are reviewed by our compliance team within 48 hours
   - Flagged content is investigated and corrected

3. Data deletion:
   - Users can delete their account and all AI-generated content via Settings → Delete My Data
   - 30-day deletion SLA (GDPR/CCPA compliant)
```
