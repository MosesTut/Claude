## Full Description (≤4000 characters)

```
Plan trips faster with Timbuktoo, an AI-powered travel concierge that creates personalized itineraries based on your preferences.

Discover destinations, restaurants, and activities with day-by-day plans designed to help you explore confidently.

FEATURES
• AI-generated travel itineraries tailored to your interests
• Destination recommendations based on your preferences
• Local food and activity suggestions
• Weather-aware planning
• Built-in feedback tools for content quality

HOW IT WORKS
1. Share your travel preferences (food, activities, budget, dates)
2. Get personalized city recommendations
3. Generate complete day-by-day itineraries
4. Explore with confidence—meals, activities, and local tips

WHY TIMBUKTOO
• Save hours of travel research
• Get recommendations that match YOUR interests
• Discover local favorites, not just tourist traps
• Plan entire trips in minutes, not days

AI DISCLOSURE
This app uses artificial intelligence to generate recommendations. AI-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete and is provided for informational purposes only. Users should independently verify important details before making travel decisions.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Your information is encrypted and used only to personalize your experience
• No tracking or advertising
• You may request deletion of your data at any time from Settings
• SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure
• See our Privacy Policy for full details: https://timbuktoo.ai/privacy

DATA SAFETY
We collect:
• Email address (for account creation)
• Travel preferences (for personalization)
• Usage data (for app improvement, not linked to you)

We do NOT collect:
• Location data (GPS)
• Contacts, photos, or other personal files
• Financial information (payments handled securely by Google)

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).

SUPPORT
Questions or feedback? Email support@timbuktoo.ai
Privacy inquiries: privacy@timbuktoo.ai

Terms of Service: https://timbuktoo.ai/terms
Privacy Policy: https://timbuktoo.ai/privacy

Follow us:
```

## Post-Submission

**Google Review Timeline**: 1-7 days (average 2-3 days)

**Internal Testing Requirement**:
- Minimum 20 testers
- Minimum 14 days of testing
- After 14 days, eligible for Production release

**If Rejected**:
1. Read rejection email carefully
2. Address specific Policy violation
3. Update app or listing as needed
4. Reply in Play Console with explanation
5. Resubmit

**Common Rejection Reasons** (and how we've addressed them):
- ❌ "Data Safety form incomplete" → ✅ All data types declared, form filled accurately
- ❌ "AI usage not disclosed" → ✅ Full description includes AI disclosure section
- ❌ "Privacy Policy missing data deletion" → ✅ Privacy Policy includes deletion workflow
- ❌ "No demo account" → ✅ reviewer@timbuktoo.ai credentials provided
