==============================================================================
AI DISCLOSURE COMPLIANCE
==============================================================================

User Notifications:
-------------------
1. ONBOARDING SCREEN (Screen 2 of 3):
   - Title: "AI Transparency"
   - Message: "Timbuktoo uses artificial intelligence to generate travel recommendations. These recommendations are informational and should be independently verified."
   - MANDATORY CONSENT CHECKBOX: "I understand itineraries are AI-generated"
   - Users CANNOT proceed without checking this box

2. IN-APP BANNER (Every Itinerary):
   - Banner displayed prominently at top of every itinerary
   - Text: "⚠️ This itinerary was generated by AI"
   - Yellow background (color: #fff3cd, border: #ffc107)

3. FEEDBACK MECHANISM (Required for AI Apps):
   - Every itinerary has "Give Feedback" button
   - Options:
     • 👍 Helpful / 👎 Not Helpful
     • ⚠️ Report Issue (Inaccurate, Inappropriate, Missing Info, Other)
   - All reports reviewed by compliance team within 48 hours

==============================================================================
Content Moderation:
-------------------
- Users can report inaccurate or inappropriate recommendations
- All reports stored in database with user_id, itinerary_id, report_type, comments
- Compliance team receives Slack alert for "inappropriate" or "inaccurate" reports
- Flagged content reviewed within 48 hours
- Inaccurate content removed from knowledge base
- Inappropriate content escalated immediately

Safety Measures:
----------------
- Source attribution required (OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, TripAdvisor)
- Trust tier filtering (≥70% tier 4-5 sources)
- Hallucination detection (cross-check with vector database)
- 5% sample rate for human review (high-cost trips > $0.60 AI cost)

DEMO FLOW (Recommended Test Path)
==============================================================================

1. LAUNCH & ONBOARDING (2 minutes)
   --------------------------------
   Step 1: Open app → Splash screen (2 seconds)
   Step 2: Onboarding Screen 1: "Welcome to Timbuktoo"
           - Shows 3 features (AI-Powered, Discover Cities, Day-by-Day)
           - Tap "Continue"

   Step 3: Onboarding Screen 2: "AI Transparency" ⚠️ CRITICAL
           - Shows AI disclosure message
           - Checkbox: "I understand itineraries are AI-generated"
           - Try tapping "Agree & Continue" WITHOUT checking box
             → Alert: "Consent Required" (confirms blocking works)
           - Check the box
           - Tap "Agree & Continue"

   Step 4: Onboarding Screen 3: "Your Privacy Matters"
           - Shows data use summary
           - Links to Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
           - Tap "Get Started"

2. AUTHENTICATION (1 minute)
   --------------------------
   Option A: Use test account
   - Enter email: reviewer@timbuktoo.ai
   - Enter password: ReviewTest2024!
   - Tap "Log In"

   Option B: Create new account
   - Tap "Don't have an account? Sign up"
   - Enter email: [your email]
   - Enter password: [8+ characters]
   - Confirm password
   - Tap "Sign Up"

3. PREFERENCES (2 minutes)
   ------------------------
   - Select interests (e.g., "Food & Dining", "History & Culture", "Nightlife")
   - Select food preferences (e.g., "Local Cuisine", "Street Food")
   - Select budget: "Moderate ($$)"
   - Optional: Enter travel dates (e.g., "June 2025")
   - Tap "Find My Perfect City"

4. CITY RECOMMENDATIONS (30 seconds)
   ----------------------------------
   - Loading screen: "Finding your perfect cities..." (5-10 seconds)
   - 3 cities displayed with reasoning (e.g., "Bangkok, Thailand")
   - Each city has "Build My Trip" button
   - Tap "Build My Trip" on first city

5. ITINERARY GENERATION (30-60 seconds) ⚠️ TAKES TIME
   ---------------------------------------------------
   - Loading screen: "Crafting your perfect trip..."
   - Subtext: "This may take 30-60 seconds"
   - Progress indicator (spinner)

   ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Do NOT interrupt this process. The app is calling multiple
   AI agents sequentially (Intent Parser → City Selection → Local Expert →
   Tools → Concierge → Post-Processor). This is expected to take 30-60 seconds.

6. ITINERARY VIEW (2 minutes) ⚠️ AI DISCLOSURE BANNER
   ----------------------------------------------------
   - AI DISCLOSURE BANNER displayed at top (yellow background):
     "⚠️ This itinerary was generated by AI"
   - Title: "Bangkok" (or selected city)
   - Subtitle: "7-Day Itinerary"
   - Day-by-day cards (Day 1, Day 2, etc.)
   - Each day shows:
     • Weather (e.g., "Sunny, 32°C")
     • Meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner with venue names, cuisine, price tier)
     • Activities (time, name, description, address with 📍 icon)
   - Scroll through all days
   - Tap "Give Feedback" at bottom

7. FEEDBACK (1 minute) ⚠️ REQUIRED FOR AI APPS
   --------------------------------------------
   - Title: "How was your itinerary?"
   - Rate as "👍 Helpful" or "👎 Not Helpful"
   - Report Issue section:
     • Inaccurate information
     • Inappropriate content
     • Missing key information
     • Other issue
   - Optional comments text box
   - Tap "Submit Feedback"
   - Alert: "Thank You. Your feedback helps us improve recommendations."

8. SETTINGS (2 minutes) ⚠️ DATA DELETION REQUIRED
   -----------------------------------------------
   - Navigate to Settings (from Preferences or Itinerary screen)
   - Sections:
     • Account (Email, Log Out)
     • Legal (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service - both open in browser)
     • Support (Contact Support, Report a Bug)
     • About (App Version 1.0.0, AI Disclosure)
     • Danger Zone: "Delete My Data" button (red)

   - Test Data Deletion:
     • Tap "Delete My Data"
     • Dialog: "Delete Account?" with list of what will be deleted
     • Warning: "This action cannot be undone. Deletion will be completed within 30 days."
     • Tap "Cancel" (to avoid actually deleting test account)
       OR
     • Tap "Delete Everything" to test full deletion flow
       → Loading spinner
       → Alert: "Account Deleted" with confirmation
       → Redirected to Onboarding screen

PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING
==============================================================================

Data Collection (Declared in Privacy Nutrition Label / Data Safety):
---------------------------------------------------------------------
✅ Email Address: Account creation (linked to user)
✅ Travel Preferences: Personalization (linked to user)
✅ AI-Generated Itineraries: User content (linked to user)
✅ Feedback & Reports: Content moderation (linked to user, optional)
✅ Usage Data: Analytics (NOT linked to user)
✅ Crash Logs: Diagnostics (NOT linked to user)

Data NOT Collected:
-------------------
❌ Location (GPS coordinates)
❌ Contacts
❌ Photos
❌ Microphone/Camera
❌ Payment Info (handled by Stripe, not stored by Timbuktoo)
❌ Device IDs or Advertising IDs (no tracking)

Data Security:
--------------
✅ Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS)
✅ Encryption at rest: AES-256 (AWS KMS)
✅ Secure storage: Keychain (iOS), Keystore (Android) for JWT tokens
✅ JWT expiration: 30 days
✅ Annual penetration testing (May 2024: 0 critical/high findings)

Data Deletion:
--------------
✅ User-initiated: Settings → Delete My Data
✅ Deletion SLA: 30 days (GDPR/CCPA compliant)
✅ Confirmation email sent upon completion
✅ All data deleted: account, preferences, itineraries, feedback

Third-Party Sharing:
--------------------
✅ Anthropic: Travel preferences (NOT email) for AI itinerary generation (DPA signed, SOC-2 certified)
✅ AWS: All data (encrypted) for hosting (DPA signed, SOC-2/ISO 27001 certified)
✅ Datadog: Usage logs (PII masked) for monitoring (DPA signed, SOC-2 certified)
❌ No data sold to advertisers or data brokers

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
==============================================================================

Platform: React Native 0.72 + Expo 49
Authentication: JWT tokens (30-day expiration, stored in SecureStore)
API: https://api.timbuktoo.ai/api/v1/
- /auth/signup, /auth/login, /auth/logout
- /preferences (GET/POST)
- /cities/recommendations (GET)
- /itinerary/generate (POST) ⚠️ Takes 30-60 seconds
- /feedback (POST)
- /user/account (DELETE)

Dependencies:
- React Navigation (screen navigation)
- React Native Paper (UI components)
- Expo Secure Store (token storage)
- Axios (HTTP client)

Permissions:
- Internet access (network requests to API)
- No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone permissions required

iOS Build: Xcode 15+, iOS 13+ minimum
Android Build: API Level 23+ (Android 6.0+) minimum
